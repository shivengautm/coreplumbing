Common Questions

Quick answers before you book a callout.

Yes — all of our engineers are fully Gas Safe registered. You can verify this on the official Gas Safe Register. We'll always show our ID and registration card before starting any gas work in your home.
We aim to respond as fast as possible to all enquiries. For urgent issues like boiler breakdowns, gas leaks or burst pipes, same-day callouts are available subject to engineer availability. Call us directly for the fastest response.
Absolutely. We provide CP12 Gas Safety Certificates for landlords across West Yorkshire. This is a legal requirement for all rental properties and must be renewed annually. We can usually fit you in quickly and provide the certificate the same day.
We work with all major boiler brands including Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Baxi, Ideal and Main. New boiler installations come with manufacturer warranties of up to 10 years depending on the brand and model chosen.
We work on fixed-price quotes wherever possible. Before any work begins, we'll give you a clear price breakdown so there are no surprises on your invoice. What we quote is what you pay — no hidden call-out charges or extras.
